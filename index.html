08 / Good questions

A little clarity.
Before we begin.

Something else on your mind?
Include it in your estimate request.

What painting services do you offer?

Interior and exterior painting, cabinet refinishing, popcorn ceiling removal, epoxy garage floor coatings, drywall repair, carpentry and small remodels, plus commercial painting for offices and retail spaces. Pressure washing and full surface prep are part of every exterior.

Do you handle carpentry repairs too?

Yes. Rotted fascia and soffit, damaged trim, baseboards, door frames and custom millwork are repaired or replaced by our own crew before the paint goes on, so you never need a second contractor.

How do I get a free estimate?

Fill out the short form below or give us a call. We’ll schedule a visit, walk the project with you, and send a clear written estimate with no obligation.

How long will my project take?

Most interiors take a few days and most exteriors about a week, depending on size, repairs and weather. We’ll give you a schedule up front and keep you posted along the way.

Can you help me choose a color?

Absolutely. We’ll bring samples, paint test patches on your actual walls and talk through what works with Florida light, your roof, your neighbors and any HOA guidelines.
