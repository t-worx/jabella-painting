## The eight topics

1. **Vibe.** Authored: clean, confident, coastal, premium-local. References:
   the Evercoat page itself (structure and pacing), a Boca Raton Mediterranean
   revival street at golden hour, a crisp Benjamin Moore fan deck.
2. **Scroll journey.** Cloned from the reference, in order: scrubbed painting
   film hero, trust strip, three services, before/after slider, inspiration
   pair, why-us dark band, four-step process, about with team photo, promise
   quote, FAQ accordion, estimate form, footer.
3. **Energy curve.** Loud open (the film), settles through services and the
   slider, quiet in the dark band, warm in about, still at the promise quote,
   practical at the form.
4. **Feeling curve and the peak.**
   - Hero: anticipation. A can opens, a brush cuts a line, a house glows.
   - Services: orientation. Three clean cards.
   - Before/after: the peak. The same Highland Beach house, dragged from faded
     and mildewed to fresh white with navy shutters. The visitor says: "you
     drag the house from tired to brand new."
   - Inspiration: desire.
   - Why band: reassurance.
   - Process: clarity.
   - About: warmth.
   - Promise: stillness.
   - FAQ: confidence.
   - Estimate: action.
5. **One thing no site does.** Authored: the hero film is the visitor's own
   hand; scrolling is the brush stroke. Kept from the reference by request.
6. **Range.** Premium-minimal, as the reference is.
7. **One world or distinct scenes.** Distinct scenes. The hero film is three
   cut shots scrubbed as one timeline, like the reference.
8. **Assets.** Logo only (304x93 webp, upscaled through Higgsfield). All
   photography and footage generated with Higgsfield, one style preamble
   reused verbatim.

## Brand kit

- Logo: `assets/logo.webp` (blue wordmark, black tagline, roller icon).
- Palette from the logo: blue `#0A62C4`, black `#0B0F14`. Authored
  supporting tones: canvas `#F6F7F9`, surface `#FFFFFF`, muted `#EEF1F5`,
  body `#5B6572`, border `#DDE2E8`.
- Blue takes every role Evercoat gives gold (italic accents, kickers, number
  markers, focus ring). Black takes every role Evercoat gives navy (dark band,
  headings, buttons, footer).
- Type: Archivo for display, Inter for text, Instrument Serif italic for the
  accent words. All from Google Fonts.
