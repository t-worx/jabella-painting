Your cabinets are sound. They just need a fresh face.

Our 4-step process

How we get a factory-smooth finish.

  1. 01

    Deep cleaning and degreasing

    We remove years of built-up grease and grime to ensure perfect adhesion.

  2. 02

    Sanding and prep

    All surfaces are scuff-sanded and any damage is repaired for a smooth foundation.

  3. 03

    Professional priming

    A bonding primer is applied to guarantee the topcoat adheres and lasts.

  4. 04

    Spray application

    We spray multiple coats for a factory-smooth, brush-mark-free finish.
