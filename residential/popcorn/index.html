Why remove popcorn ceilings?

Six reasons homeowners call us.

  • Outdated look that dates your home
  • Collects dust, cobwebs and allergens
  • Difficult to paint or repair
  • Can contain asbestos in pre-1980s homes
  • Reduces light reflection in rooms
  • Lowers home resale value

Safety first

We test before we touch it.

Homes built before 1980 may have asbestos in their popcorn texture. We always test before starting work and follow all EPA guidelines to keep your family safe. Your health and safety are our top priority.

Our process

Four steps to a smooth ceiling.

  1. 01

    Testing and safety

    We test for asbestos before any work begins. If found, we follow EPA-certified abatement procedures.

  2. 02

    Room preparation

    Furniture is moved, floors and fixtures are covered with protective sheeting.

  3. 03

    Texture removal

    The popcorn texture is carefully scraped away, then surfaces are sanded smooth.

  4. 04

    Skim coat and paint

    A thin skim coat creates a perfectly flat surface, followed by a fresh coat of ceiling paint.
